TuxBooks 0.1.1 makes the EPUB progress bar something you can drive. Click
to jump, drag the thumb to scrub, and step with the keyboard once the
thumb has focus. The fixes around it keep a seek from flashing through a
foreign position and keep keyboard reading on the new position after a
jump. PDF scrolling tracks your drag on mixed-size pages again, and the
settings dialog loses the library actions it repeated plus the vague
navigation labels.

## New

- Click the EPUB progress bar to jump to that rough book position, drag the thumb to scrub, and step it from the keyboard. A label above the bar reads the position Kindle-style while you move it: `Location 1508 of 5913 • 26%`.

## Fixed

- The EPUB progress bar no longer flashes through a foreign position while a seek settles.
- Arrow-key steps reach the EPUB engine again after a jump, so keyboard reading continues from the new position.
- The PDF scrollbar thumb no longer leaves the cursor during a drag on documents with mixed page sizes. Every page is measured up front instead of lazily.
- Rapid scrolling no longer cancels render work that was already committed, so pages stop blanking mid-scroll.
- The settings General tab drops the library actions it repeated from the sidebar.
- The settings navigation uses plain names: Reading, Data, and Folders replace the vague labels.

## Install on Ubuntu/Debian

```sh
sudo apt install ./tuxbooks_0.1.1_amd64.deb
```

Or run the portable AppImage:

```sh
chmod +x tuxbooks-0.1.1.AppImage
./tuxbooks-0.1.1.AppImage
```

`SHA256SUMS.txt` verifies the downloads. The SBOM is published next to
the installers.
